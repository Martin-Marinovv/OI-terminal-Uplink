ASSETS — what goes in the assets/ folder

assets/terminal-frame.png — your terminal frame image. Must be 1920×1080px. The frame image sits on top of all content. The transparent/cut-out area in the middle of the image is where the data panels and video/message show through. To use a different terminal in the future, just replace this one file.

assets/lory-uplink.mp4 — your video file. Rename it to match exactly.
